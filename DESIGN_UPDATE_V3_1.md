# Plekxa Corporate v3.1 — Clean White Design

This release simplifies the corporate website around a clean, image-led entertainment-company layout.

## Design changes
- White navigation and footer
- One clear homepage hero rather than overlapping visual elements
- Large editorial image-and-copy feature blocks
- Restrained typography, spacing and buttons
- Simplified newsroom cards
- Clean white treatment across Company, Products, Careers, Events and Newsroom
- Existing Contact page structure and darker visual direction retained

## CMS connections retained
- Homepage hero and purpose: `cms_homepage_sections`
- Newsroom and article pages: `cms_articles`
- Company page: `cms_pages`
- Leadership: `cms_leadership`
- Careers: `cms_jobs`
- Events: `cms_events`
- Header/footer navigation: `cms_navigation`
- Footer settings: `cms_settings`
- Contact form: `support_requests`
